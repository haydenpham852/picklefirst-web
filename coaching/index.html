Questions
Who are the coaches?

Local coaches Hayden plays with. English-speaking, matched to your level. You are told who before you pay.

What happens in a first session?

10 minutes of play, then drills on the two things costing you most points, then live points.

Can I do two hours a day?

Yes. 2 back to back = 2 credits, same coach.

Only here a few days?

Book 1 hour, or the 5-hour pack as 2 + 2 + 1.

Why cheaper than home?

Coaches and courts cost less in Vietnam. $22 to $27 is about a third of a US or Australian lesson.

Do I need a paddle?

Bring one if you have it. If not, say so and the coach brings a spare.

Can I pick the coach?

Yes, ask in the notes. You can also switch after the first hour.
